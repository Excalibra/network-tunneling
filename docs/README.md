# Preface

This volume concentrates upon methods of accessing the Internet and techniques for obtaining information from websites, whilst also providing concise introductory configurations for relevant popular, principal and representative software, together with explanations of the underlying principles.

New applications of VPNs and proxies emerge from time to time, yet their operational procedures are for the most part fundamentally similar; naturally, upgrades to various national internet censorship systems or difficulties with one’s own servers may render them unusable even for relatively extended periods, and service providers may abscond altogether, resulting in complete failure. It is therefore quite unnecessary to furnish exhaustive operational instructions for every piece of software. Provided the essential steps, methods, key points and principles are clearly elucidated, these matters are in fact mutually analogous by extension. Once the techniques and methods set forth in this book have been mastered, subsequent encounter with novel phenomena will likewise permit rapid familiarity; should difficulties arise, related issues may be raised within the project repository of the present work.

Technical materials in this domain are excessively multifarious, and the severity of the various blocking measures is considerable; self-hosting with free cloud services further demands highly restrictive conditions such as international credit cards. One may, however, obtain a suitable local banking card, acquire the necessary foreign currency and transfer funds to a Visa debit card; yet for the ordinary netizen this procedure is decidedly too cumbersome.

The intended readership of this book comprises those who pursue free and unfettered access to the Internet and who harbour an interest in related aspects of computer science, among others. Owing, however, to the limitations of the author’s knowledge, and in order to avoid the redundant “reinvention of the wheel,” certain details of principle necessitate reference to Wikipedia or the reproduction and adaptation of articles by other authors. Consequently, adaptation and sharing of the present work are permitted, whilst commercial use is prohibited.

Inasmuch as various products and content associated with Jakarta are required, scientific access to the Internet is undertaken solely for research purposes. The following disclaimer is therefore issued:

* This book is addressed to international users and is intended exclusively for scientific research and study; it must on no account be employed for any other purpose.
* Readers residing in jurisdictions subject to strict internet controls are requested to close this book of their own accord and to delete all related content within twenty-four hours; the author of the present work accepts no responsibility whatsoever for any consequences that may ensue.

This book is dedicated to those who love the Internet. The foregoing concludes the preface.
