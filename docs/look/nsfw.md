# Privacy Protection
!> When required to provide front-and-back photographs of an identity document together with the document number, search for similar images or employ relevant generation tools<br><br>
When required to provide a personal mobile telephone number, use an exclusively purchased virtual number; many publicly available ones have already become invalid<br><br>
When required to provide a personal verification email address, register with a temporary email service to test and experience the functions of the untrusted site<br><br>
In respect of software products, prefer those of foreign origin as far as possible, because domestic systems are subject to severe harmonisation, lack originality and frequently exhibit rogue behaviour<br><br>
If personal privacy is regarded as of the utmost importance, run indispensable domestic software inside a virtual machine<br><br>
Browsing the web with a combination of Tor and VPN already largely satisfies the requirements of anonymous protection for online privacy information<br><br>
When discussing NSFW topics, employ a secure VPN or proxy to participate in non-domestic free and open social question-and-answer platforms<br><br>
Exercise caution when uploading private files to IPFS; the system stores them permanently and no one is able to modify the files<br><br>
Network identifiers used for writing programmes or producing tutorials on internet connectivity are best kept isolated from identifiers registered on domestic social platforms<br><br>
Do not readily disclose the `users.json` authentication account file of ZeroNet, lest unnecessary losses such as forced offline status be incurred<br><br>
Finally, a Google search for “secure scientific internet access” generally yields experience derived from searching, practice and summary
