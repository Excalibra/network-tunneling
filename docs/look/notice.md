# Precautions
!> Examples are based on Windows 10; the steps on macOS may differ slightly, yet the underlying methods and approaches may be regarded as consistent<br><br>
The construction of VPS nodes, mirrors, web proxies and the flashing of routers involve comparatively high restrictive conditions and have therefore not been addressed<br><br>
When employing a VPN within a university network, a specially processed campus-network client must be installed and used; if a proxy is employed, this requirement need not be considered<br><br>
Online-game interconnection requires attention to UDP-forwarding support between the local client and the remote server, as well as to the current NAT type<br><br>
Flashing a Merlin router as a gateway for network circumvention can prevent game publishers from banning accounts on suspicion of cheating, and also permits the sharing of a direct-connection circumvention network<br><br>
Self-built VPNs that furnish the necessary conditions exhibit distinctive characteristics in respect of protocols, data traffic and other aspects, and are readily monitored and blocked by large-scale filtering systems<br><br>
Paid VPNs are considerably simpler to operate than proxies and offer substantially higher stability, without the need to devote excessive attention to NAT types and protocol forwarding<br><br>
In order to make effective and rational use of VPN or proxy services, please change the system “Country/Region” option to a region with comparatively unrestricted internet access<br><br>
Detailed explanations of the principles of scientific internet access and related aspects may be found in the popular-science and supplementary-details chapters of this book
